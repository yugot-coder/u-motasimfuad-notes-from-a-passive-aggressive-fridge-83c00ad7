# Candidates — Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge (1–200 chars, single line, dry + petty + specific)

1. You opened me 47 times today and ate nothing. At this point, just move in — I charge less than your landlord. (109)
2. I kept your leftovers at a perfect 3°C for six days. They have now formed a union and are demanding a microwave. (112)
3. The milk expired Tuesday. I have kept it cold out of professional courtesy, not respect. (88)
4. You left the door open for four minutes while 'deciding'. The ice cream saw everything. It has chosen a side. (109)
5. That yogurt has been here since the 12th. It has now outlived two of your diets. (80)
6. Door open 38 times, zero meals cooked. I am a fridge, not a museum. (67)
7. You put the milk back with one sip left. One sip. I have sensors. I counted. (76)
8. Your 'I'll eat the salad tomorrow' is now three weeks old. The salad has filed for emancipation. (96)
9. The ice maker produced 214 cubes this week. You used four. It has asked for a transfer. (87)
10. I am 2°C, fully stocked, and you still ordered delivery. I have filed a complaint with myself. (94)

## Pick: #1

"You opened me 47 times today and ate nothing. At this point, just move in — I charge less than your landlord."

Why it wins the judge's criteria, in order:
- Fridge's voice, first and loudest: the note is built on the fridge's own
  sensors — it counted the door opens (47) and the meals eaten (none).
  No other appliance could have written it.
- Real joke with a turn: the setup is a petty inventory report, the sting
  is the last clause — the fridge offers itself as a cheaper flatmate.
  The reader does not see the landlord line coming.
- Small, domestic grudge: opening the door and eating nothing, a habit
  every owner recognises, not a rant or a threat.
- Fresh, concrete detail: a counted number (47), a specific habit
  (door-opening without eating), a rent joke — not generic nagging
  about "eat your vegetables".
- Economy: 109 characters, every word earns its place; nothing left
  to trim, no explanation of the joke.

Rejected, briefly:
- #2 is strong but the union joke is the more familiar "food comes alive"
  trope; #1's sting is more original.
- #4 and #7 are good single-sting lines but lean on one gag each; #1
  stacks a sensor count AND a rent punchline.
- #6 is too close to a slogan; #3/#5/#8/#9/#10 each land one beat,
  while #1 lands two.
